THE METHODOLOGY

Two formulas. One clear decision.

01 / EARLY READERS

Spache Grades 1–4

Revised Spache estimates a reading grade from average sentence length and the proportion of distinct unfamiliar words in successive 100-word passages.

0.659 + 0.121 × ASL + 0.082 × PUW
02 / DEVELOPING READERS

Dale–Chall Grades 5–12

The classic formula evaluates unfamiliar-word occurrences and sentence length, then translates the result to a two-grade reading band.

0.1579 × PDW + 0.0496 × ASL + adjustment*

* Add 3.6365 when difficult-word percentage exceeds 5%. Uses the 1995 familiar-word list with the classic equation and grade bands. This is not the separate 1995 cloze-table procedure. Readability is not the same as curriculum, content or pedagogical appropriateness. Poems, dialogues, short samples and poorly extracted PDFs need additional review. Screening thresholds are editorial guidelines, not standardized pass/fail benchmarks.
