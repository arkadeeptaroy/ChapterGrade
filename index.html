Scoring methods

Spache Grades 1–4

Revised Spache estimates a reading grade from average sentence length and the proportion of distinct unfamiliar words in successive 100-word passages.

0.659 + 0.121 × ASL + 0.082 × PUW

Dale–Chall Grades 5–12

The classic formula evaluates unfamiliar-word occurrences and sentence length, then translates the result to a two-grade reading band.

0.1579 × PDW + 0.0496 × ASL + adjustment when applicable
